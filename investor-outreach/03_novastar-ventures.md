African employers — from SMEs to large enterprises — have no HR infrastructure built for their context. Global tools assume IBAN payroll, single-country tax law, and English-only compliance. African businesses need multi-currency, multi-country, and local labour law compliance from day one.

We're building the Africa Operating System — a platform OS for African employers.

**What we have working:**

The core workforce layer is complete:
- Multi-tenant org structure (legal entities → locations → departments → teams → positions)
- Employee records with full lifecycle: onboarding, active, on leave, terminated
- Compensation history (multi-currency, audit-complete)
- Secure employee document management with encrypted storage and time-limited access URLs
- Granular permission system (18 permissions) mapped to HR roles: HR Admin, Manager, Employee, Tenant Admin
- Append-only compliance audit log — every action is recorded, immutable, and tenant-isolated

The data model is designed for Africa: currency is stored in minor units per ISO 4217 to handle CDF, NGN, KES, GHS, ZAR without rounding errors.

**Why now:** African SME formalization is accelerating — governments are pushing digital payroll reporting, mobile money is replacing cash wages, and compliance audits are becoming real consequences. The window to own this infrastructure layer is open.
